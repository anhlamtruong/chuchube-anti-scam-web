# Tagline options

The site ships with **"Thấy giả? Chuchube!"** / **"Spot a fake? Chuchube!"** as the hero headline.
These are alternatives for the owner to choose from. Each keeps the rallying cry "Chuchube!" (you
spotted a fake: block and report it right away). The English lines are faithful renderings, not
word-for-word translations.

## Tiếng Việt

1. **Giả mạo là Chuchube liền.** Short, a rule of thumb: fake means Chuchube, right now.
2. **Nghi giả? Kiểm tra, rồi Chuchube!** Names the first step (check) before the cry.
3. **Trang thật hay trang giả? Hỏi Chuchube.** Leads with the question users actually ask.
4. **Một ảnh chụp, biết ngay thật giả.** The product promise without the cry; good as a subline.
5. **Thấy giả đừng lướt qua. Chuchube!** Asks the reader not to scroll past a fake.

## English

1. **If it's fake, Chuchube it.** Turns the cry into a verb.
2. **Doubt a page? Check it, then Chuchube!** Names the first step (check) before the cry.
3. **Real page or fake? Ask Chuchube.** Leads with the question users actually ask.
4. **One screenshot. Real or fake, with reasons.** The product promise without the cry; good as a subline.
5. **Don't scroll past a fake. Chuchube!** Asks the reader not to scroll past a fake.
